Volumes Échanges RE Dashboard

Exploring aggregated volumes exchanged among French “Responsables d’équilibre” (RE).

This dashboard visualizes commercial transactions performed by French responsables d’équilibre in various mechanisms (PEB RE-RE, ARENH, VPP, Bourse, etc.) between the years available. Hover over the bars to see exact volumes in MWh. All data is pulled directly from the official ODRE dataset.

Below, you'll find two stacked bar charts: one for the “av_...” (average volume) columns, and another for the “ij_...” (index volume) columns. Filters and selection can be performed by toggling chart legend items. A complete data table is also provided for reference.
