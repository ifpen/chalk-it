Electricity Consumption Dashboard (soutirages-regionaux-quotidiens-provisoires-rpt)

This interactive, purely front-end dashboard showcases daily and half-hourly electricity consumption across different regions, sectors, and voltage levels. It is built as a storytelling experience: we begin with high-level trends over time, then drill down into specific regions and sectors.

1) Overview of Daily Consumption

This line chart shows the total daily consumption (sum of énergie_journalière) across all regions.

2) Regional Comparison & Sector Analysis

Select a date to view the regional breakdown (bar chart) and sector breakdown (pie chart). This helps identify which regions and sectors are driving consumption on a given day.
