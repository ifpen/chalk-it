bp2017-elec-parc-de-production Dashboard

This interactive dashboard explores the “bp2017-elec-parc-de-production” dataset, illustrating future electricity production capacities under multiple RTE scenarios (Ampère, Hertz, Volt, Watt) for different horizons (2025, 2030, 2035). It uses data published on the ODRE platform and can help stakeholders and researchers understand how France’s electricity mix could evolve, comparing nuclear, renewable, and thermal capacities.

Adjust filters below to explore total installed capacities over time and view a detailed breakdown by technology for any specific scenario-year.
