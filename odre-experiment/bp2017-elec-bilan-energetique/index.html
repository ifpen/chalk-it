BP2017 Elec Bilan Energétique Dashboard

This interactive dashboard explores France’s future electrical system under different scenarios (Ampère, Hertz, Volt, Watt) for 2025, 2030, and 2035. It highlights key indicators such as consumption, CO₂ emissions, and share of nuclear vs. renewables in order to present a “story” of how the French electrical mix could evolve.

Scenarios

Ampère, Hertz, Volt, Watt

Horizons

2025, 2030, 2035

Key Measures
