This dashboard provides insight into France’s monthly electricity consumption based on raw (brute) data vs. weather-corrected (corrigée) data, along with the share consumed by directly connected clients (large-scale industrial usage). Brute consumption represents raw measurements, while corrected consumption adjusts for seasonal and weather fluctuations. Directly connected clients can indicate the portion of usage from major industrial consumers.
