Volumes Actives MA Dashboard

This dashboard visualizes electricity volumes and costs from France’s “mécanisme d’ajustement” (MA), using open data from RTE (via Opendatasoft). It covers volumes activated upward/downward, average costs, and the number of half-days under “situations tendues.”

Key Highlights

Volumes Over Time

The first chart shows the total downward (negative) vs upward (positive) volumes by year. A large negative total means more electricity was asked to reduce production (or increase consumption), while a large positive total means more was asked to produce more/consume less.

Average Costs Over Time

The second chart shows the average cost of downward/upward activation in €/MWh. Under tension, these costs can change significantly.

Situations Tendues by Year

This chart shows how many half-days were marked as “tensed” for both baisse and hausse. A higher count may correlate with spikes in cost or volume adjustments.

Filière Contributions

Select a year to see how different production types ("filières") contributed to the downward/upward volumes. This helps identify whether hydraulique, nucléaire, thermique, etc., were most used for adjustments.

Data Source: Opendatasoft (RTE)
