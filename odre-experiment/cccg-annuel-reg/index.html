Dashboard: CCCG Annual Gas Consumption

Welcome to the CCCG (Centrales à Cycle Combiné Gaz) Annual Consumption Dashboard. This dashboard showcases how gas consumption has evolved over different years. Explore the dataset below to see high-level trends, key statistics, and interactive filtering.

The data is fetched live from the OpenDataSoft “cccg-annuel-reg” dataset. Please note that the real dataset may include more records, possibly with regional details. For this example, we focus on the annual consumption (in MWh) and highlight key figures.
